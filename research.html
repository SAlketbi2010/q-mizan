05 · Research

What we build on

Q-MIZAN joins two fields that have not met yet: quantum gravity sensing and carbon mineralisation.

Timeline

Key breakthroughs

  • 1992

    First cold-atom gravimeter

    The first absolute gravimeter that uses laser-cooled atoms instead of a solid falling mass.

    Exail review, 2024
  • 2008

    Peridotite locks up CO2 naturallyUAE

    Kelemen and Matter show that peridotite in the Oman–UAE mountains turns CO2 into carbonate surprisingly fast, and that the process could be engineered.

    PNAS, 2008
  • 2015

    Quantum gravimeters go on sale

    The first commercial transportable quantum gravimeter; units are now used in hydrology and volcanology.

    Exail, EGU 2024
  • 2021

    Gravity tracks stored CO2

    Repeated borehole gravity surveys follow CO2 being injected into, then removed from, a reservoir in the USA.

    Geophysics, 2021
  • 2021

    Clogging and cracking

    Lab experiments show carbonation of peridotite clogs pores but also cracks the rock, which can keep the reaction going.

    Phys.org, 2021
  • 2022

    Quantum gravity goes outdoors

    A quantum gravity gradiometer from the University of Birmingham finds a tunnel underground in real-world conditions.

    Nature, 2022
  • 2023–24

    Fujairah pilotUAE

    ADNOC and 44.01 mineralise 10 t of CO2 in Fujairah’s peridotite in under 100 days, then announce a 300 t scale-up.

    Gulf News, 2024
  • 2025

    Scaled-up Fujairah siteUAE

    44.01 begins injecting at a scaled-up site, with one borehole estimated at 25,000 t of CO2 a year.

    44.01, Dec 2025
  • 2025

    Gravity sees total mass

    A study finds gravity monitoring of stored CO2 is sensitive to the total mass injected, however it spreads.

    Scientific Reports, 2025
  • 2026

    Cement CO2 to rockUAE

    Holcim UAE and 44.01 commission a pilot to mineralise CO2 captured at a Fujairah cement plant.

    MEED, 2026

Glossary
Peridotite
A dark-green rock from the Earth’s mantle, common in the Hajar mountains, that reacts with CO2.
Mineralisation
The reaction that turns dissolved CO2 into solid carbonate minerals inside rock.
Gravimeter
An instrument that measures the strength of gravity at one place.
µGal (microgal)
A unit of gravity: one billionth of 10 m/s². Earth’s gravity is about 980 million µGal.
Superposition
A quantum state in which an atom is in a combination of two possibilities until it is measured.
Interference
What happens when two waves meet: they add up or cancel, depending on how they line up.
MRV
Measurement, reporting and verification: the proof needed before stored carbon counts.
TRL
Technology Readiness Level, a 1 to 9 scale of how close an idea is to real use.
